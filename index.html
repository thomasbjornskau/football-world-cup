    About the data. Historical results are compiled manually from public records (FIFA archives, the open openfootball dataset and the Fjelstul World Cup Database). Early tournament formats are mapped to the nearest modern stage, and knockout matches decided on penalties are counted as draws. Tactical notes are curated summaries of openly available analysis — indicative, not definitive. This is a prototype covering a small set of teams.
